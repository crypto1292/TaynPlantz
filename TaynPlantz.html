Plant Resource Guide

Helpful information for plant owners

💧

Watering

Don't automatically water on a fixed schedule. Check the soil first. Many indoor plants prefer the surface to dry somewhat between waterings.

☀️

Lighting

Bright indirect light works well for many houseplants. Keep plants with intense light requirements closer to suitable windows while avoiding sudden changes.

🌱

Soil & Drainage

Containers should have appropriate drainage. Choose soil based on the plant's needs rather than using one mix for every species.

🪴

Repotting

Repot when roots become crowded, the soil structure has deteriorated, or the plant has clearly outgrown its container. Avoid unnecessarily oversized pots.

🐾

Pet Safety

Plant toxicity varies by species and sometimes by plant part. Always verify a plant with a trusted veterinary or poison-control resource before assuming it is safe for pets.

🔬

Identification Tips

For the best scan, photograph one plant clearly. Include leaves, flowers, fruit or bark when possible. Good lighting and a sharp image can improve identification.
