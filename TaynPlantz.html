Plant Resource Guide 🌿

Practical plant-care information organized by topic.

☀️ Light

Light requirements vary significantly by plant. Bright indirect light works well for many indoor plants, while some plants tolerate lower light and others need several hours of direct sunlight.

Watch the plant rather than relying only on a window's direction. Stretching, pale growth and small new leaves can indicate insufficient light, while scorched or bleached patches can indicate excessive sun.

💧 Water

Check the soil before watering. Many houseplants do better when the upper portion of the potting mix dries before the next watering.

Avoid leaving plants sitting in standing water unless the species is specifically adapted to wet conditions.

🪴 Soil & Drainage

Container plants generally benefit from a potting mix appropriate to their species and a container that allows excess water to drain.

Dense or continually saturated soil can reduce airflow around roots.

💨 Humidity

Tropical plants often appreciate higher humidity, but increasing humidity does not replace appropriate light, watering and airflow.

🌱 Feeding

Fertilizer requirements depend on species, growing conditions and season. Follow the fertilizer's label and avoid assuming that more fertilizer means faster or healthier growth.

🩺 Common Problems

Yellow leaves, wilting, leaf spots, pests and sudden growth changes can have several causes. Use the plant's identification and growing conditions together when troubleshooting.
