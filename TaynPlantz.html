🌿 Plant Care Resources

☀️ Light

Bright indirect light works well for many indoor plants. Direct afternoon sunlight can scorch sensitive leaves.

💧 Water

Check the soil before watering. Many houseplants prefer the soil to partially dry between waterings.

🌱 Soil

Good drainage is important. Choose a soil mix appropriate for the specific type of plant.

🌡️ Temperature

Most tropical houseplants prefer stable indoor temperatures and should be protected from cold drafts.
